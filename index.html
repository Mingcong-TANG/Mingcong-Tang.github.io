Welcome!

Hi, this is Mingcong 😊

I am an incoming Ph.D. student in Clinical Psychology, working with Dr. Qimin Liu at the Quantitative Psychopathology Lab in the Center for Anxiety & Related Disorders (CARD) at Boston University. Previously, I worked with Dr. Lily Brown at the Center for the Treatment and Study of Anxiety (CTSA), Perelman School of Medicine, University of Pennsylvania. I also worked with Dr. Todd J. Farchione at the TREND UP Lab in CARD.

My research examines affective dynamics and emotion regulation processes underlying depression and anxiety. I use multi-method approaches across multiple levels of analysis, combining intensive longitudinal designs with quantitative and computational modeling to characterize how symptoms emerge, fluctuate, and persist over time. My aim is for this dynamic, mechanistic work to advance more precise assessments that capture what we actually intend to study, elucidate meaningful and quantifiable heterogeneity in how these conditions are expressed—affectively, cognitively, and symptomatically- and inspire staged, targeted, and mechanism-informed interventions.

Beyond my research, I gained clinical and translational experiences across academic medical centers, private clinics, and mental health startup settings in China and North America, where I worked with children, adolescents, young adults, and families and contributed to the development, cultural adaptation, and digitalization of family-systems-informed assessment and interventions.
